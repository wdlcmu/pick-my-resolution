The method, in six steps

Step 1

Pick one, not a list

One goal gets your whole week behind it. A list splits that week five ways. Splitting is how good plans starve.

Step 2

Size it to your real week

Thirty minutes a week is a real plan. So are two hours a week. Pick the time you truly have, not the time you wish you had.

Step 3

Start with one small first move

Week one asks for one small move, not a new life. Here is a real pick from the quiz, word for word.

Step 4

Plan for the hard day now

Hard days are part of the year. Pick your counter before you need it. The four counters live on our why resolutions fail page.

Step 5

Check in once a week

Ask two short questions. Did I do my move this week? What got in my way? Your pick comes with a prompt you can copy into your own AI.

Step 6

Write a note to future you

Tell future you why this one matters. Save the note as a letter card. Read it when the year gets loud.
