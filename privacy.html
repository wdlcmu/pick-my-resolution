Privacy

Your quiz plan stays in your browser.

Cloudflare and PostHog count visits to this site.

There is no account or email signup.

What the quiz saves

Finishing the quiz saves your four answers and result.

The result includes its check-in and share text.

The save uses this browser's local storage.

Your answers and plan stay on this device.

Anyone using this browser can open it.

The quiz code uses local storage, not cookies.

The result screen also offers a letter card.

Writing a note saves that note too.

It saves the open date you picked.

Your note is never sent to us.

Clearing your saved plan

Open your saved plan and tap Start over.

That button removes the saved answers and result.

It removes your saved note too.

Browser site-data controls can remove it too.

Copying and sharing

Copy buttons place text on your clipboard.

The site does not send copied text anywhere.

You choose where to paste copied text.

Sharing starts only when you tap Share.

Site counts

This page loads Cloudflare Web Analytics.

It sends page and performance counts to Cloudflare.

Cloudflare says its beacon uses no cookies.

Cloudflare says its beacon does not read local storage.

The home page also runs PostHog.

PostHog counts page views and quiz starts.

It counts finishes, shares, and return visits.

It counts check-in copies, lock-screen pictures, and letter cards.

Your note text is never sent to it.

Your quiz answers are never sent to it.

It does not record your screen.
