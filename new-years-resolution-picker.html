The science under the plan

Four findings from real research shape every plan here:

  • If-then plans. Gollwitzer: "If 9am, then I walk" beats trying.
  • Tiny first steps. BJ Fogg: start so small it is hard to skip.
  • The fresh start effect. Dai, Milkman, and Riis: a new year lifts motivation.
  • Weekly check-ins. Watching your own record keeps the streak alive.
